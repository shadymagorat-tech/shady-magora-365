For owners

How it works, in plain words.

Short version: I run your listing on Airbnb. Your own team runs the place itself. Here's how it splits, and how we start.

Message me on WhatsApp

What I do

Everything that happens on Airbnb.

  • A listing that gets clicked

    Title, description, photo order and amenities, written in English, French and Arabic so travellers from everywhere understand it.

  • Prices and calendar

    Nightly prices that move with weekends, Eid, Christmas and the beach season, inside the minimum you set.

  • Guests, day and night

    Fast replies before, during and after every stay. House rules explained before arrival, not after a problem.

  • Reviews, disputes and claims

    When something goes wrong, I know the Airbnb process from the inside: reviews, disputes and AirCover claims.

  • Clear payouts

    A plain statement of every booking and your share, settled every month.

What I don't do

I manage the listing, not the building. Nothing on the ground is included, so you and your team arrange these before the first booking:

  • Cleaning and laundry
  • Key handover
  • Guest check-in and check-out
  • Repairs and maintenance
  • Anything else that needs someone at the property

How to start

  1. Message me on WhatsApp

    Tell me the area and send a few photos. The button below opens a message that's ready to go.

  2. A short intro

    We talk about your place, the lowest nightly price you accept and the dates you want to keep for yourself. [TO CONFIRM: on WhatsApp, a call or a meeting?]

  3. A written agreement

    A signed agreement in Arabic and English before anything goes live. [TO CONFIRM]

  4. Your listing goes live

    I write or rework the listing in English, French and Arabic, set the prices and calendar, and start answering guests. [TO CONFIRM: you add me as co-host on your own Airbnb listing, or another setup?]

  5. Monthly payouts

    A plain statement of every booking and your share, settled every month.
